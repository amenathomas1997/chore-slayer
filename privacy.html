Chore Slayer — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-24

Chore Slayer is a household chore-and-activity tracker used by a parent or caregiver to manage tasks for the children in their family. This policy explains what the app stores and how it is handled. The app is operated by Full Circle AT LLC ("we") and your data is stored on our own private server at chores.a-t-365.com.

What we store

  • Children's first and last names, plus an optional icon/color, entered by the parent.
  • Chores and activities and their schedules.
  • Daily check-offs (which tasks were completed each day).
  • Daily reports a parent may write: mood, a 1–5 behavior rating, highlights, challenges, notes, and goals.
  • A parent PIN, stored only as a secure one-way hash.
  • If you turn on notifications: a push token for that device or browser, plus which kid's reminders it gets and whether it gets parent alerts. The token is only used to deliver Chore Slayer notifications.

Apart from that optional push token, we do not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, or advertising data, and the app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

How we use it

The information is used solely to provide the app's features for your household — showing each child their tasks, tracking completion, and producing the parent and BHIS reports. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

How it's shared

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties. Data stays on the operator's server. (If you self-host, you are the operator and controller of that data.)

Notifications. If you turn them on, the notification text (for example a chore's name and a child's first name) is passed to the delivery service for your device: Apple Push Notification service on iPhone and iPad, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android, or your web browser's push service. They only deliver it. Signing a kid out stops that kid's reminders on the device, unlinking the device or deleting the family removes its push token from our server, and tokens a device no longer uses are deleted automatically.

Children's privacy

Chore Slayer is a tool for parents and caregivers. A parent enters and controls all children's information behind a PIN; children only tap to check off their own tasks. We collect the minimum needed (a name) and never use children's data for advertising or tracking.

Retention & deletion

Parents control the data at any time: removing a child hides them but keeps their reports for your records, and "Delete forever" permanently erases that child and all of their tasks, check-offs, and reports. To delete everything, a parent can choose "Delete family" (in the app's parent menu, or on the website) and confirm with the PIN: the family and all of its children, tasks, check-offs and reports are permanently erased from our server right away. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. The parent PIN is stored as a secure hash, repeated wrong-PIN attempts are rate-limited, and parent actions require a signed-in session.
