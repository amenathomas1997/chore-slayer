Getting started

  1. Open Chore Slayer and choose Create a family. Pick a parent PIN (4 to 8 digits).
  2. Write down the Family Code you're shown. You need it to add another device or sign in on the web.
  3. Tap Add a child, then open the child and add chores and activities.
  4. Tap Kid sign-in and let your child type their first and last name to see their list.

Common questions

How do I add another phone, tablet or the website?

On the new device choose Connect this device and enter your Family Code and parent PIN. On the web, go to chores.a-t-365.com. A parent can see the Family Code from the ⋯ menu → Family code.

Can my kids get into the parent settings?

No. The parent area needs the PIN. It signs out when the app closes and after 10 minutes without use.

I forgot my PIN.

Email us from the address you'd like us to reply to, with your family name. We'll verify the family with you before resetting anything.

How do I delete my family and all its data?

Sign in as a parent, open the ⋯ menu and choose Delete family…, then enter your PIN to confirm. Everything is erased from our server right away and cannot be undone.
